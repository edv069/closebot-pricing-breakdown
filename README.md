## What a "message" really costs after the discount

The subscription is only one line in your bill, and the discount-hunting usually stops right before the part that decides whether the plan is cheap or expensive.

**Free plan:** 100 messages included monthly, then $0.08 per message if you keep going. CloseBot's own advice is that if you're regularly over 100, you want a paid plan.

**Business plans:** your message ceiling is 500 included at the base tier, and you can raise it monthly — the higher the ceiling, the better the bulk rate. Go over the ceiling and you pay per message at a 2x overage rate, drawn from a wallet you top up.

**Agency plans:** you pay per message and mark it up. Here the sources disagree: the current plans page FAQ says agencies are billed a flat **$0.012 per message**, while CloseBot's help doc still says **$0.006 per message**. That's a 2x gap on the number your margin is built on, so verify it in your own account before you quote a client a price based on either figure.

Seats and storage are the other two meters, and both are the same on business and agency plans: one user included, **$5 per additional seat**, one agent included, storage as an add-on. Business storage add-ons run from $0.10 to $3.00 per MB per month depending on how much you buy; agency storage is metered at $0.006 per MB per day. For scale, CloseBot notes 1 MB of text is roughly 1,000 pages.

One definition worth pinning to your wall: **1 message = 1 segment**, unless you switch on the Agent Node's "unlimited potential" mode, where billing shifts to token costs and one user message can consume several segments.

## The costs hiding behind the subscription

**The CRM underneath.** CloseBot is CRM-native. It doesn't connect to Instagram or WhatsApp itself — it takes over the text channels inside your CRM. CloseBot integrates natively with HighLevel and HubSpot, plus custom CRMs. If you already run one of those, the CloseBot subscription is your only new cost. If you don't, a review published in August 2026 points out that GoHighLevel's entry plan starts at $97/month, which changes the total considerably. A "discount" on a $64 subscription means nothing if you're adding a $97 platform to use it.

**Your AI provider on agency plans.** CloseBot's docs state that V2 requires you to bring your own provider keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Grok or DeepSeek) and that CloseBot doesn't cover the token costs. CloseBot's own pricing comparison puts token cost at roughly **$0.0025 to $0.01 per message** depending on provider, and notes DeepSeek runs well under OpenAI's rates. On business plans, CloseBot's blog says message *and* token costs are included at the $64 entry tier — no metered provider bill. Contradicting this, the plans page FAQ says CloseBot doesn't allow bring-your-own-key at all for security reasons. Ask support which applies to the exact plan you're buying; it's the single biggest swing in ownership cost.

Worth reading those two paragraphs twice, because they're the difference between "CloseBot is $64 a month" and "CloseBot is $64 a month plus provider tokens plus a CRM."
